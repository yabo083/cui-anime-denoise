3. Real-CUGAN weights (up2x-latest-denoise3x.pth, optional model)
   Source   : https://github.com/bilibili/ailab (Real-CUGAN)
   The weights are NOT redistributed with this program. When the user picks
   the CUGAN model the app downloads the upstream release archive, verifies
   the published SHA-256, and extracts the .pth into
   %LOCALAPPDATA%\AnimeDenoise\models. The bundled
   models\cugan_up2x_denoise3x.skel.onnx is a weightless graph that reads the
   downloaded .pth as external data. License terms follow the upstream
   repository.

   (SHA-256 verified); models\apisr_rrdb_x2.skel.onnx is the weightless graph.
